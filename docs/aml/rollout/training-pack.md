# Role-specific training pack — synthetic pilot (E11)

Short, role-scoped briefings. One rule binds every role: **no
organisation's approval makes another organisation compliant.** The
Command Center originates and evidences; every partner retains its own
determination, monitoring, records and regulatory responsibility. Never
state or imply otherwise, in any channel.

Universal prohibitions (all roles): never disclose risk ratings/scores,
potential matches, screening or adverse detail, analyst/reviewer/MLRO or
EDD reasoning, suspicious-matter or AUSTRAC information, legal holds,
internal discrepancies, or security incident details — to anyone outside
the origin's restricted roles. Safe status codes and fixed copy only.

## Origin analyst
Work the intake/verification queues as before — nothing about partner
visibility changes your workflow. Partner-facing events are automatic and
safe-worded; you never write partner-facing copy. Records requests from
partners land in the MLRO review queue, not yours. If a partner contacts
you directly for case detail: decline, point to their workspace, log it.

## Origin reviewer
Your reasoning, notes and overrides are P4 — structurally non-exportable.
Material changes you record can flag attestations for refresh; the safe
reason code is all a partner ever sees. Legal holds you impose block
disposal and are never partner-visible.

## Origin MLRO
You own: arrangements + regular reviews (overdue blocks new grants),
attestation issuance/material change, grants (issuance flag-gated;
revocation always available), records-request review (approve EXACT codes
only), delivery recording (attach only ACCEPTED case documents), disposal
approval/execution. Every outward disclosure decision is yours and logged.
Decision-register items D1–D10 need your recorded decisions before
sign-off.

## Origin auditor
Everything is reconstructable: hash-chained case events, the evidence
access log (every attempt, approved or denied, with safe codes), outbox
event evidence (never deleted on success), disposal receipts with content
hashes. Deep-links in queues reproduce the filtered record sets.

## Finance / builder / solicitor OPERATIONS users
You can view the workspace, prepare information and see safe statuses. You
CANNOT record determinations or retrieve delivered evidence — those need
your organisation's compliance officer role. That denial is correct
behaviour, not an error.

## Finance / builder / solicitor COMPLIANCE OFFICERS
The workspace shows what the issuing organisation DID (procedures), never
what it CONCLUDED. Your determination is your organisation's own decision
and never changes the origin case. Requests use the closed record-code
catalogue; delivered evidence opens only after you record a retrieval
reason, via a link that expires within minutes — request again if it
lapses. A "refresh required" banner means re-review and re-record; a
"temporarily unavailable" record means exactly that and nothing more.
Solicitor-specific: matter files, privileged material and internal legal
content never enter this workspace in either direction.

## Platform support
First moves live in `support-escalation-matrix.md`. You never read AML
case data to answer a ticket; denial codes in the access log are your
diagnostic surface. Anything smelling like restricted-info exposure is
SEV-1: flag off, revoke, preserve, escalate.
