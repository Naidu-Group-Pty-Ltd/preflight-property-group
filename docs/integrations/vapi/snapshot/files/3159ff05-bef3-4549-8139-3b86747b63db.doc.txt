The Rebrand  
From Agile In Tandem to Unstructured
--------------------------------------------------

"We're ditching the Agile safety blanket."

That's right, we've made a bold move. As of September 2024, "Agile In Tandem" is dead. Long live "Unstructured"! 

​

Why the change? 

​

Agile is excellent, but it's not the be-all and end-all: We've realized our impact goes beyond fancy sprint boards and daily stand-ups. Teams are the real MVPs: We're diving deep into what makes them tick, and spoiler alert - it's not always about story points.

​

Our new approach? It's as unconventional as a pineapple on pizza. We're tossing out the rulebook and embracing authenticity and adaptability. No more one-size-fits-all solutions - we're going to be entirely bespoke.

Breaking free from the Agile straitjacket allows us to tailor our services faster than a TikTok trend. It's all about flexibility, honesty, and results that mean something.

So, welcome to Unstructured to all our clients - past, present, and future.

Where the only structure is no structure at all.
