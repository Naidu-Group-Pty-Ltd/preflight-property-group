1. Introduction: Who We Are
NPC Services (Naidu Property Consulting Services) is a nationally operating, full-service property
consulting and buyer’s agency that delivers a seamless, end-to-end investment experience for clients
across Australia. Our mission is to simplify the property journey, provide strategic clarity, and create
long-term wealth through sustainable, scalable, and data-driven investment pathways.
Backed by strong partnerships with finance specialists, accountants, developers, builders, and property
managers, NPC Services acts as your single point of contact for everything you need — from your first
strategy session to post-purchase support and portfolio expansion.
We are more than a buyer’s agent. We are your lifelong investment partner.

5. Long‑Term Scaling: How We Help Clients Recycle Equity Every
12 Months
Most investors struggle to grow beyond their first or second property. NPC Services specialises in
helping clients scale:
How We Achieve This:
Initial Purchase – Secure a strong investment that grows quickly and performs from day one.
Equity Check (6–12 months) – Review valuations to identify usable equity.
Refinancing Pathway – Work with finance partners to restructure loans for maximum leverage.
Cashflow Strengthening – Optimise rental returns, reduce outgoings, and position clients for
the next acquisition.
Subsequent Purchase Strategy – Source the next opportunity using equity rather than savings.
Outcome:
Clients are able to purchase consistently, with many building portfolios of 2–4 properties within 2–3
years, subject to lending criteria and financial stability.
NPC Services ensures your money continues working — again and again.
